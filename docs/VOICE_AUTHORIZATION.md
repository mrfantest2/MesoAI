# MesoAI voice authorization gate

MesoAI may prepare, normalize, rank, hash, and evaluate private source audio without publishing it.

Generation of a cloned/synthetic voice for a real person remains disabled until the project records that the target person authorized this use.

Required record before synthesis:

- target profile: `meso`
- target person: Maissoun
- authorized use: local/private voice cloning and synthesis for MesoAI voice-fidelity testing
- authorization confirmed: `true`
- evidence/note: a concise statement describing how authorization was obtained
- recorded timestamp

This gate is intentionally independent from memory/persona work. Voice authorization does not authorize publishing private WhatsApp content, exposing raw audio, or enabling memory/persona inference.
